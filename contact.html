🛡️ DMCA & Community Removal Protocol

If you are an official admin, owner, or legal representative of a community indexed on SaaS Customer Success & Churn Reduction Hub and wish to modify your listing or have your channel removed from our public index, please select 'Listing Removal / Takedown Request' above.

We process all verified owner removal requests within 24 business hours with zero friction. You may also report broken invite links, updated community descriptions, or ownership changes directly through this portal.

Official takedown notices must clearly identify the specific URL listing in question, state proof of authorized administrative ownership, and specify the exact rectification desired.

⏱️ Editorial Service Level Agreements (SLA)

Our operational infrastructure adheres to strict service level benchmarks to support user trust and webmaster collaboration:

  • 24-Hour Editorial Review: General inquiries, broken link notifications, and submission revisions are acknowledged within one business day.
  • Priority Copyright & DMCA Handling: Formal intellectual property notices receive prioritized attention within 12 business hours.
  • Direct Email Inquiries: Inquiries may also be routed directly to our designated compliance desk at desk@authoritydirectory.org.
  • Security & Abuse Resolution: Reports of compromised channels or malicious behavior result in instant provisional quarantine pending manual investigation.

🔒 Security Vulnerability & Safe Harbor Policy

We take the security and integrity of our directory ecosystem seriously. If you are an independent security researcher or developer who has identified a potential vulnerability, broken routing endpoint, or data integrity issue on our site, we invite you to submit a coordinated disclosure report.

Our engineering team investigates all legitimate reports within 24 hours. We operate a good-faith safe harbor policy: we will not pursue legal action against ethical researchers who discover issues without disrupting live operations, scraping private user data, or violating platform terms.
