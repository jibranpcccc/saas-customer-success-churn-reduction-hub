📝 Community Information

📋 Community Curation Standards & Acceptance Criteria

Our editorial team applies strict, transparent standards to maintain our reputation as an authoritative directory. Before submitting your community, ensure it complies with the following mandatory prerequisites:

  • Active Membership Threshold: Communities must demonstrate a minimum of 250 verified participants with regular conversational velocity. Stagnant groups or bot-inflated channels are automatically disqualified.
  • Anti-Spam & Moderator Presence: Dedicated administrators or automated moderation bots must be active to remove unsolicited promotions, harmful links, and abusive interactions.
  • Zero-Cost Accessibility: Primary discussion rooms must be 100% free to access. We do not index groups that require paid subscriptions, private NFT gates, or upfront transactional fees to enter.
  • Permanent Public Direct Links: Invite links must point directly to official platform endpoints (such as discord.gg vanity links, t.me channels, or reddit.com subreddits) without intermediate shorteners or paywalls.

🔍 The Automated Verification Pipeline

Every submitted group enters our automated curation pipeline to confirm its validity prior to publication:

Phase 1: Automated Endpoint Ping: Our system verifies that the provided invite link returns a valid HTTP response and is not expired, rate-limited, or redirected.

Phase 2: Editorial Review: A human reviewer inspects the community rules, description accuracy, and member engagement to ensure alignment with our directory taxonomy.

Phase 3: Directory Publication & Indexing: Approved communities receive live cards with real-time copy invite buttons, spec matrix badges, and structured Schema.org breadcrumb markup.
